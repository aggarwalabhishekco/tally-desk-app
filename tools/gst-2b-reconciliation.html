Input Tax Credit Reconciliation Engine
Your GSTR-2B and Books files never leave your browser. Both files are read and matched entirely on your own device using JavaScript — the files themselves are never uploaded to any server, including ours. (A short contact form below is the one exception — see it for details.) This tool does not ask for, and will never ask for, your GST portal login or OTP. Covers regular B2B invoices and credit/debit notes (B2B-CDNR/B2B-DNR) from GSTR-2B; it does not cover amendments (B2BA/CDNRA/DNRA), rejected entries, ITC-reversal entries, ISD credits, or import of goods (IMPG) — check those sheets in your GSTR-2B separately.

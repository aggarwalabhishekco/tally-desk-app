Capital Gains Tax Calculator — FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)
Educational tool — not a filed computation. This reflects capital-gains rules as amended by the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 (effective for transfers from 23 July 2024): simplified 12-month/24-month holding periods, 20% STCG / 12.5% LTCG (no indexation) on listed equity, and 12.5% LTCG (no indexation) on other long-term assets — with the option to use 20% with indexation only for land/building bought before 23 July 2024. It does not cover every asset class (e.g. depreciable business assets, slump sale) or set-off/carry-forward of capital losses. Please have us verify your actual computation before filing.

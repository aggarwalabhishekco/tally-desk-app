TDS Rate Finder & Calculator — FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)
Educational tool — not a substitute for professional advice. TDS is now governed by the Income Tax Act, 2025, in force from FY 2026-27 (1 April 2026), which consolidates almost all non-salary TDS into a single Section 393 — the old section numbers (194A, 194C, 194J, etc.) still map cleanly onto it and are shown alongside the new Section 393 reference and TDS reporting code below. Thresholds and rates carry over largely unchanged from the position under the old Income-tax Act, 1961 (as last amended by Budget 2024 and Budget 2025). The specific numeric TDS reporting codes and Table Sl. No. sub-references shown could not be independently confirmed against a primary CBDT source at the time this tool was built — verify these specific codes against the actual TRACES/e-filing return utility before using them in a filing; the section, threshold and rate figures are more reliably sourced than the codes. Always confirm with us before deducting, especially for large or unusual payments — this is still a very new law and some fine print is still being clarified by the department.

Quick-Reference: TDS Sections Covered
Disclaimer: This is a self-help reference and calculation aid, not a certified TDS determination. It covers common resident-payee sections only — payments to non-residents (Section 195 under the old Act), TDS on salary (Section 192 / new Section 392, slab-based), and TDS on immovable property (Section 194-IA) are not covered here. Old-Act thresholds and rates reflect our understanding following Budget 2024 and Budget 2025 changes; the new Section 393 references and TDS codes reflect the Income Tax Act, 2025 as understood shortly after it took effect for FY 2026-27, and a few Table Sl. No./code mappings (flagged above) are still being confirmed as CBDT issues further clarifications. Aggarwal Abhishek & Co. does not certify these figures — please confirm with us before deducting or filing your TDS return.

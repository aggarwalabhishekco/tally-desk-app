Break-Even & Cash Runway Calculator
Planning aid, not an audited metric. Break-even uses the standard contribution-margin formula (Fixed Costs ÷ (Price − Variable Cost per Unit)). Runway assumes your current net monthly burn rate stays constant — real burn usually changes as revenue and costs grow, so treat this as a snapshot, not a fixed forecast.

Step 1 — Export From Tally

In Tally: open the company → Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Trial Balance (and separately, Day Book) → press Alt+E → Export → Format: Excel (Spreadsheet) → save the file.

The Day Book is optional but recommended — it lets the tool list your actual debtor/creditor/vendor names and flag large cash transactions, which the Trial Balance alone (just group totals) can't show.

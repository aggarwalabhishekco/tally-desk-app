Valuation & Dilution Simulator
Planning aid, not a term-sheet model. This uses standard priced-round dilution math (New Investor % = Amount Raised ÷ Post-Money Valuation) and models an ESOP pool top-up as happening pre-money (the common market practice, where the pool expansion dilutes existing shareholders, not the incoming investor). It does not model convertible notes/SAFEs, liquidation preferences, anti-dilution protection, or secondary sales — real term sheets can change outcomes materially.

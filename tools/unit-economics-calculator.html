Unit Economics Calculator — CAC, LTV & Payback Period
Planning aid, not an audited metric. This calculator uses the standard, widely-used unit-economics formulas (CAC = spend ÷ new customers; LTV = ARPU × gross margin % × average customer lifetime). The LTV:CAC benchmarks shown (3:1 as "healthy") are commonly-cited investor rules of thumb, not a guarantee — a good ratio for your specific business depends on your industry, funding stage and growth ambitions.

    Disclaimer: This is a self-help planning aid, not an audited or certified metric.
